Research overview

ICML 2026

Revealing Long-context Potential of Attention Heads via Frequency Kernels

Senyu Han, Yilu Cao, Kai Yu, Lu Chen

This work introduces a Long-context Potential Score (LPS) by analyzing the static frequency kernels of attention heads. It relates parameter-level structure to long-context behavior and explores efficient, training-free interventions.
